01 The Rundown

Black Friday game plan
(even if you waited 'til now)

We'll make this simple. If your churn's under 5%, skip the fancy funnels, crazy paid-in-full discounts, and “buy my Black Friday that doesn't work” goo-roo nonsense.

Curiosity wins

In gyms like ours, simple, curiosity-driven messaging tends to outperform everything else when it comes to Black Friday. The emails and texts in here never say what the offer is (on purpose). The details go in the reply/conversations you have in your gym.

Your most powerful move: a reactivation

With “never been” and “used to be” clients. A “curiosity” message driving to an actual sales convo has been our most effective method of getting new (and winning back past) clients by far.

The audience

  • ✅ Never been clients
  • ✅ Past clients
On GMM / Alloy Ops?

Just let the team know if you'd like us to set this (or any) Black Friday reactivation up for you and we'll take it from there!

Watch first

Two videos in Circle cover this whole window: The Big Picture (Black Friday & Holiday Fun) and Winter is Coming: how to think (and act) to grow in Q4 (watch that one on 1.5-2x, it's a beast). The written play is the lesson this page is built from: Black Friday Summary + Reactivation Options.

What actually gets you members:

You decide the actual offer BEFORE Email 1 goes out, because the first reply you send is the details.

One person answers every reply, same day, and books the convo while they're hot.

The offer ends when the last-call text says it ends. No quiet extensions.

02 Before You Discount

Read this before you
give away a paid-in-full

I hesitate to overuse the word need… but you should damn well probably do something :) People are in buying moods, have their credit cards out, ready to go, you might as well capitalize on it 🤷‍♂️

The most common (short sighted) crazy stuff

We all know the most common thing you see is “Oh I gotta make a ton of money on Black Friday, better give away my memberships as a paid in full.” I don't hate you if you've done it, and there's a lot of good that can come of it, but also a lot of bad…

Most folks want a couple things on BF (let's be honest everyone wants this w/ everything in their lives): lots of money w/ as little effort as possible. This leads most folks down a rabbit hole of doing larger PIF packages with some fancy funnel so you don't have to actually talk to anyone to make the sale… you end up w/ 6m-12m options or 12-12-12 [access differentiated] options…

If you're not priced to go out of business you're probably looking at a bare minimum of $1200 (that should really be minimum $3k+ w/ most of you doing smaller group stuff). Who's gonna pay a $3k ticket price without talking to anybody beforehand? Your current members! So you run a deeply discounted PIFFY play to try to make it enticing for them so that someone buys on Black Friday and you can experience the (admittedly awesome) joy that comes from someone buying off a “funnel.” Sounds cool, but in practice... 

This can be fine if…

  • You're desperate for cash and are gonna close the doors if you can't make rent in December
  • You've got a ton of renewals coming up b/c you've done this in past years and might have trouble w/ conversions w/o a PIFFY push
  • You're incredible at paid acquisition and you just need access to more cash to push more traffic through the system (e.g. you can predictably replace the lost EFT w/in 30-60 days)
  • You've been swindled by a guru company to run a PIF promo so you can pay their fee /sarcasm (don't get me started)

The bad news?

  • If your attrition % is lower than the discount %, you lose cash over time (if you discount 10%/m but you only lose 3% of members every month, you done lost money, amigos)
  • If you're a bad steward of your cash (not an unlikely scenario if you ran this b/c you're desperate for it) you could just be speeding up the downfall of the biz
  • If the goal is new members, the likelihood of getting someone off the street to buy a big PIFFY package w/o speaking to you is incredibly low (so you miss out on new member growth & cash that you could prob use this time of year)

So while you might get to say “OMG I made $15k on Black Friday…” you mighta just lost a big ole hunk of money in the long run :(

Gonna run a PIFFY campaign anyway? Do it smart
  • Externally, drive to a conversation (not a checkout page). Keep things vague until you can get prescriptive on exactly what that person needs and then present the PIFFY options.
  • Internally, drive for bonuses and value-adds first, discounts second (or not at all). I'd MUCH rather eat a little payroll w/ some bonus 1-on-1s or free access to group/scalable bonuses, discounts on merch or supps, etc. than cannibalize cash flow w/ PIFFY discounts (PARTICULARLY if your retention is great).
  • Discounted upgrades for PIFs are muuuuch more fun than bread & butter membership discounts and one of my favorite ways to leverage increased purchasing from current members on BF without hurting your current recurring revenue for said members. 

Summary: best bet for non-members is a curiosity-driven Black Friday offer leading to what is essentially your standard sales process in a Black Friday wrapper

That's the reactivation on this page. Add bonuses or PIFFY deals on new traffic if you're "screw it" priced (most of you aren't) or you're well-versed in higher-ticket ($2k+) one-time sales + walk-downs. If you don't know what that means, don't do it. 

If you must "funnelize" your Black Friday, LBOs are probably your only bet to have any success externally (sorry). We can set up fun fancy pages and all the bells and whistles in the world, but none of you SHOULD be priced at “eh, screw it, let's try it” pricing, so you're better off driving to a conversation vs. driving to a checkout page. Have cash (if running ads), capacity, and proven conversion rates from your LBO to EFT? Then sure, but I'd MUCH prefer to have you run a 1-2-2 or 2-4-8 and funnelize that.

Easy options for members

  • ½ price upgrade for X weeks/months [frequency, service level]
  • PIF discount on ancillary services you already have [nutrition, PT] [5-1; 10-3; 20-6]. Cash w/o hurting bread-and-butter cash flow.
  • Retail blowout sale

Launch something new?

Black Friday is a GREAT time to launch something new. If you've been looking for an excuse (and you're actually ready) to add a nutrition, online, or other new program, it can be a great way to get folks in. Ask yourself:

  • Is your day to day already on point?
  • Do you have a “who” + process/systems to run the new thing well? Follow-up, sales, fulfillment.
  • Is the opportunity cost worth it? (e.g. if you just did more of the same for the next 6 months do you end up better off?) What's the risk of quality/attention decline if you add something new to your operation?

Bare minimum: if you only send one thing

Let's say you don't want to do anything fancy, no new programs, no crazy PIF offers, nothing for your current clients, etc. At least send this. It works as a stand-alone one-off if you're not running the 3-email sequence below.

03 Decide First

5 decisions before you
touch the copy

The messages never say the offer, the date, or the number of spots. You still have to know all three, because the first person who replies EARLY is going to ask.

1

The actual Black Friday offer

Our rec

One offer. Something w/ a real deadline that you'd be happy honoring through January. Examples include: your current offer that's working (with a Black Friday wrapper), a contrasting A-B, $X off month one w/ the start-up fee waived, a paid-in-full deal (3 or 6 months w/ a full sales conversation), or a short front-end package (free or paid) that rolls into your normal membership.

  • The membership after the offer is your normal rate. No legacy rates.
  • Write it ONE way on the cheat sheet and everybody says it that way. Two people quoting two different deals is how you lose a sale you already had.
  • Goes in the BF Offer custom value as a thing, e.g. “$150 off a 3-month membership.” No “you” or “your,” the sentence around it does that.
2

When it goes live

  • = the day Email 2 (“It's live”) fires.
  • Our rec for '26: Fri Nov 20, the Friday before Thanksgiving. Black Friday's shifted earlier anyway, and you want the convos happening before everybody leaves town.
  • Early access folks (anybody who replies to Email 1 / Text 1) get the details the day they reply. Don't make them wait for the live date.
3

When it ends

  • Email 3 says “ends tonight,” so pick the night. Our rec: Black Friday itself (Fri Nov 27) at midnight. Cyber Monday (Nov 30) if you want the weekend to close folks.
  • Say it once and mean it. Don't quietly extend it after, or the next deadline you send gets ignored.
  • Goes in the BF Offer End custom value as just the date + time, e.g. “Friday Nov 27 at midnight.”
4

How many spots

  • “Very limited number” needs a real number behind it. Pick one you can actually onboard in December w/ your model (e.g. 10 for a small group gym, 20 if you've got the room and the coaches).
  • Create an artificial cap even if you've theoretically got unlimited capacity in a large group model.
  • Goes in the BF Spots custom value as just the number.
5

Who replies to the inbound

  • Replies land in your Conversations inbox in GMM / Ops / GHL. One person owns it (owner, or whoever runs your consults). Same day, ideally within the hour.
  • Decide now: sell by chat (our default, section 08). The offer comes up when they ask or at the VIP session. Or just call them the minute they reply (great if your list is small and you've got the time).
  • Put their name + cell on the cheat sheet so the desk knows where to send “what's the Black Friday deal?”

05 Ads

3 situations

There are essentially 3 situations you'll probably find yourself in:

Have an offer that's currently working for you and you don't really care to change things? KEEP RUNNING IT! Maaaybe talk to your CS team in Slack and see if the squad thinks adding Black Friday “wrappers” might keep things rolling for you, but otherwise, if something's working, keep doing it!

Have an offer that works but want to do something for Black Friday anyway? Again, the most effective answer here tends to be adding Black Friday “wrappers” to whatever's currently working for you. Just let the team know in Slack and we'll get those up for you ASAP!

Need something new, period? We got you! Talk to the squad in Slack about best options for this time of year. The most effective answer tends to be a “curiosity” Black Friday offer (e.g. an ad without mentioning specific discounts or parameters) but other options can work depending on you, your gym, and your market. Hit us up in Slack and we'll get you up and running with the new thing long before the turkey coma hits.

06 List Setup

Build the list, publish
the workflow, launch

On GMM / Alloy Ops? Just let the team know if you'd like us to set this (or any) Black Friday reactivation up for you and we'll take it from there! Otherwise, three steps in your account:

Step 1: Setting up your list(s)

  • Go to Contacts → More Filters to begin building your list
  • Make sure to add this criteria for any reactivation you launch
    • Phone is not empty
    • Email is not empty
    • DND is not enabled for SMS
    • DND is not enabled for Email
  • Add additional filters to exclude members
    • i.e. Tag is NOT: member, active membership, etc.
  • Once you add all necessary filters, click Save as smart list and title it so you know it's the one you want to use for the campaign you're looking to launch

Step 2: Adjusting your workflows

  • Go to Automation and find the Plug and Play Marketing Calendar folder
  • Go to the subfolder of the month you're looking to launch in and click the reactivation workflow i.e. Reactivation: Dads Train Free June or August Back to School Challenge: Members
  • Once you click the workflow, this will bring you to the workflow builder/editor
  • If you do not want to make any edits to the copy, all you need to do is switch the workflow from draft to publish and click Save
  • Once the workflow is published and saved, it is ready to be launched!
  • If you do make edits, make sure to save each action you edit and save the entire workflow to keep any changes you make
  • Once you are done making changes, switch the workflow from Draft to Publish
  • Click Save and now your workflow is active and ready to be launched

Step 3: Launching your campaigns

  • Go to Contacts and click the Smartlist you created in step 1
  • Select all contacts on the page and then check the Select All box to select the entire list
  • Click the Add to Automation button ▶️
  • Action Name: Add to reactivation
  • Automation Type: Workflow
  • Workflow: (select the reactivation campaign you want to launch i.e. Reactivation: Dads Train Free June)
  • Send in drip mode
  • Select desired start date and time
  • Batch Quantity: 2
  • Repeat After: 1 Minutes
  • Send on: Select desired days i.e. Monday-Friday
  • Process from: select desired start and end times i.e. Start 9AM, End 6PM
For this play: the November subfolder, the Black Friday reactivation workflow. The 3 emails + 3 texts in section 07 are what should be in it. If your account's copy looks different, paste these in (Step 2 is where you edit), set the waits to match your dates (4 days between Email 1 and Email 2, 7 days between Email 2 and Email 3 on the '26 timeline), and save each action.

08 Handling Replies

Every reply starts a
sell-by-chat convo

EARLY, IN, “what is it?”, a thumbs up. Same move for all of them, same day: answer like a human, ask what they're working on, and run the GMM Sell-By-Chat Playbook. The offer does not lead. It comes up when they ask, or at the VIP session. That's the whole point of switching this to sell by chat.

The flow

  1. Pattern interrupt + the goal question. “Hey {{contact.first_name}}, {{user.first_name}} from {{location.name}} here. Saw your reply come through! What's the big goal, what are we working on?”
  2. Discovery. Let them talk. Short questions. “Have you been trying anything lately or just getting started?” Find the real friction (accountability, not knowing what to do, time, an injury).
  3. Relate. “Totally get that, we hear that all the time.” You're not selling yet.
  4. Position coaching in one line. “That's actually why most folks work with a coach here, structure and accountability instead of guessing.”
  5. Invite the VIP one-on-one. “Easiest next step is a VIP one-on-one with a coach. We'll talk through your goals and your situation and whittle the dozens of options we have down to the one or two that might make sense for you. Are mornings or evenings usually easier?”
  6. Two times, then confirm. “We could do tomorrow at 6 or Saturday at 9.” Then “Got you down for Thursday at 6. I'll text directions and a quick intro video so you know what to expect!”

Don't sell the membership in chat. Sell the session. The Black Friday deal gets presented at the session w/ your normal options (section 09).

They ask about price or the offer early

Two branches. Pick yours in Decide First and everybody on staff uses the same one.

“Screw it” priced or free offer

Just throw it out there. “It's {{custom_values.bf_offer}}, it ends {{custom_values.bf_offer_end}}, and we've got {{custom_values.bf_spots}} spots. Should I save a spot for you?” Then right back to the goal question so the convo keeps going.

Closer to normal rates, or a standard offer

“We've got lots of options. We're offering a minimum of [MINIMUM $ OR % OFF] off our programs, but the first step is a free VIP one-on-one with a coach. We'll talk through where you're at now, where you're trying to go and whittle the dozens of options we have to help folks down to the one or two that might make sense for you. Quick question - what time were you planning on training with us? Mornings, evenings, etc.?”

They push on price a second time

Doctor/mechanic frame from the playbook: “Good question. It depends a bit on what you're trying to accomplish, injury issues, scheduling, etc. Kind of like a doctor or mechanic, they need to see what's going on before recommending the right plan. That's what the first free session is for - zero pressure and the whole goal of that session is to point you in the right direction whether we ever work together 'officially' or not.” Don't dodge it a third time, be a human and give a range in the lowest increments you can (typically per session). 

They go quiet

Next day: “Hey [NAME], just wanted to make sure you saw this.” Then: “Quick question, were you still looking for [help w/ the thing they said]?” Then: “If now's not the right time no worries, just let me know 🙂” That last one gets replies surprisingly often.

Past client replied

Swap the opener for “So good to hear from you!” and use their name like you remember them (you should). Ask what changed since they left. Same flow after that.

Last day replies

Anybody who replies on [END DATE] gets the same opener plus “I can hold a spot till tonight. Got a few minutes to chat now?” Phone on you all day.

Housekeeping
  • Joined → pull them out of the workflow (or tag them so the exit condition kicks them out). Nobody who just paid should get “ends tonight.”
  • “Not right now” → “No worries at all! Want me to keep you on the list for January?” Door stays open, and they're on the Dec 7 call list.
  • Booking link is optional: {{custom_values.bf_booking_link}} if you'd rather they self-book than pick from two times.
09 The Close

Your normal sales process,
in a Black Friday wrapper

Nothing new to learn here. The session is the same session you run every other week of the year. Whoever does your consults handles these.

Run it from the Sales Training Hub

  • The Sales Process: Boot in the Door → Map the Journey → Origin Story → Ogre Onion → Transformation Vehicle → Build to Suit → Confirmation & Consent → Prescription. Scripts sit next to each lesson.
  • Presenting the offer: 1-2-2 or Contrasting A/B. The Black Friday deal is the front end, your normal memberships are the options.
  • Pushback: Objections + script.
  • Everything else: Sales Training Hub in Circle.

Past clients

Don't re-run the intro tour on somebody who trained w/ you for three years. Ask what changed since they left and what's different now. If they left over something you've since fixed (the schedule, a coach), say so. Then options.
