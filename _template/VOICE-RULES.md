G.R. rejected five drafts of the Bring Your Boo playbook in a row (Sept 2026) for "AI paced/written bullshit" and "the prose is still fucking trash." Also: **Fall-Into-Fitness-Playbook-GMM-v3 was never approved. Don't reference it.** I'd been copying its structure and voice.

**Why:** GMM's plug-and-play docs are reference guides, not essays. When I write narrative prose in his voice I produce LinkedIn-guru cadence. He notices in the first paragraph.

**What his real DFY calendar plays look like** (Drive, gr-owned: "Back to School 5 Ways to Win," "PROJECT ZERO HOLIDAY CHALLENGE REFERENCE GUIDE," "Summer Bingo Play," "March Madness Comms"):
- 5-9KB. Headers + ✅ bullets. Almost zero connective prose.
- Sections: Summary/Rundown → Prize recs → Winning criteria → Promotion (Internal / Referrals / Past customers / Your list / Cold) → Pricing → Guarantee → Talking points → Automations (emails + SMS w/ day-offsets) → Canva/tracker links.
- Placeholders are CAPS words: FIRSTNAME, GYMFRIENDLYNAME, SIGNOFF, PRICE, LAUNCHDATE. Not GHL merge syntax.
- Opinion lives in brackets/parens: "(my fave)", "booo", "[shoutout to Mike & Hunter]", "play the numbers", "one email or FB post does not = a launch", "IF I'M TELLING THE TRUTH SHOULD BE FIRST MOVE 99.999% OF THE TIME". "w/", "b/w", "e.g.", "#'s".
- Member-facing copy is warm and exclamation-y: "super fun," "pretty awesome prizes," ":)", "Let me know!", "Should I hold a spot for you?", "Check!" x3. NOT dry lowercase subjects.
- Referral tiers (2025): $50 off if avg membership < $250/m, $100 if above.

**The Circle DFY Marketing Calendar course** (gmm.circle.so/c/plug-play-marketing-calendar): March → April → May → June → July → Aug/Sept → Tech Help → Nov/Dec. **No October section.** Admin UI is fiddly to scrape (rename-on-click, upgrade modal); the Drive docs above are the same content.

**My tics to kill:** paragraphs ending on a quotable button; "X, not Y"; three-beat lists in *narration* (his own "Check! Check! Check!" in copy is fine); meta-narration; invented first-person anecdotes in his voice; 45KB of prose where 8KB of bullets was wanted.
