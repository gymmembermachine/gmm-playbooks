01 The Rundown

Don't gain weight over
the holidays, you win

A six (or eight) week holiday challenge you run for members AND open up to new folks. A weigh-in on day 1 and a weigh-in on the last day. Wraps before the New Year so your members walk into January ahead instead of starting over.

The Problem

  • Holiday sales blues (need something to make money that's not JUST a PIFFY BF play)
  • External: harder to overcome objections of holiday schedules, holiday mindset, etc.
  • Internal: lower engagement, more freeze requests, cancellations (start back up in January), etc.

The Solution

An internal/external play to get your current members fired up, grab you an excuse to get some referrals, and a built-in overcome/holiday specific external promo to get new folks in the door.

Five ways to win (where the cash comes from)

1 Current members (buy-in, waived w/ a referral)→ 2 Referrals→ 3 Past members→ 4 Your list (unconverted leads)→ 5 Cold traffic
Where it fits in Q4 (from Winter is Coming)

If you know a storm is coming it's a hell of a lot easier to prepare for it. So if we know the holidays can be tougher for churn, what are we going to do? Project Zero (or other similar plays to incentivize engagement), plus proactive goal reset convos now so they're not desperation conversations later.

You can go as "simple" as using Project Zero in Nov/Dec. Beware complexity squeeze that doesn't match the output "juice"; confusing your members is not a winning idea.

02 Decide First

5 decisions before you
touch the copy

Lock these in first. The emails, the texts, the cheat sheet and the pricing sheet all just plug them in.

1

Six weeks or eight (and the dates)

  • Six weeks = the default. Easier to sell ("just get through the holidays"), easier to staff, and it still covers Thanksgiving through Christmas.
  • Eight weeks = start two weeks earlier. Can mean revenue per outsider, more coaching, more chances to drop off. Only if your team has run a challenge before (engagement & excitement tend to drop on longer programs).
  • Either way it should end before the New Year (at minimum very close to it). That's the hook for the outsider: they start January already ahead.
2026 dates (both versions weigh out the same week)
  • 6 weeks: weigh-in Mon Nov 16 or 23→ weigh-out Mon Dec 28. Promote from Mon Nov 2 or 7. Registration closes Fri Nov 13 or 20.
  • 8 weeks: weigh-in Mon Nov 2 → weigh-out Mon Dec 28. Promote from Mon Oct 19. Registration closes Fri Oct 30.
  • Give the travelers through Wed Dec 30 to weigh out. Nobody weighs out in January unless you're pushing back a week (1/4 if running 2026 into 2027). 
  • Found this late? Shorten to 5 weeks (weigh-in Mon Nov 23) and keep the Dec 28 weigh-out or keep it at 6 and weigh-out 1/4. 
2

Member buy-in and the referral deal

  • $50-100 [play the numbers based on the optimal intersection b/w prize, buy-in, and projected participation #'s] [watch video for reference]
  • Waived with referral (a friend has to sign up for the challenge or a membership before you start)
  • Optional upgrade for lower tiered members for a fee
The referral deal (what the friend gets)

$50 off first month's membership or PZero pricing. If you've already got a referral program, use your normal Give/Get: typically $50 off if avg membership is under $250/m, $100 off if above.

3

Prize structure

✕  Pot: everyone's buy-in in a pot, winners split it

Internal only, engagement play, $50 in, all in a pot, winners split the pot. No profit to gym (booo). Only if you don't care about the money and just want engagement.

Raffle for X + EVERYONE who succeeds gets Y Our rec

  • Succeed? Name gets put in a raffle to win X (the grand prize).
  • And EVERYONE who succeeds will receive Y, where perceived value of Y is roughly = or > entry fee.
  • Works internal + external. This is what the emails on this page assume.

Alternative for new folks who succeed

Challenge fee back, given back over 12 months (i.e. as a credit on a membership). A built-in reason to stay after the challenge.

Prize ideas for X and Y are in section 04.

4

External price what a non-member pays for the challenge

I like contrasting offers incentivising memberships but it's your call.

  • What's your weekly billing rate for your avg membership? Price your challenge at a rate that makes the weekly a no-brainer, typically 7-10x your membership billed weekly e.g. if membership is $69/wk, then $499-699 nicely incentivises the membership option. $99/wk membership? $699-$999 challenge.
  • Rule of thumb: 7-10x your weekly rate. The lower you go on the challenge the less likely people are to sign up for the membership right away and vice versa.
  • Put it on the Pricing Sheet (section 12). That sheet is what you pull out at the close.
5

The guarantee: yes or no

The guarantee (section 06) is for outsiders only. It's the recommendation in the Project Zero guide, and it's the strongest risk reversal you can create at the close. 

Our rec

Run it. Only include conditions you're confident your team can execute on, because a guarantee you don't deliver on is worse than no guarantee. Decide now so every coach quotes it the same way.

03 Winning Criteria

Don't gain weight.
You win.

Don't gain weight = you win / get put in the raffle. That's it. If 100 people enter there can be 100 winners.

Weigh-in, day 1

On the gym scale, during their first session of the challenge. Coach writes it down next to their name.

Weigh-in, last day

Same scale, last week of the challenge. Second number isn't bigger than the first = they win.

Nothing else to track
  • No food logs, no apps, no points, no leaderboard. Two numbers per person.
  • Names go on the sign-up sheet on the whiteboard. Weights do NOT go on the whiteboard. Keep a clipboard at the desk (or put it in their client notes in your booking software).
  • Same scale, shoes off, don't overthink it. Equal weight counts as a win.
  • Missed the day-1 weigh-in? They've got through the end of week 1 or they're out of the raffle. Traveling at the end? Weigh out any day of the last week.

The guarantee criteria (section 06) are different and they're outsiders-only. Winning the challenge is just the scale.

04 Prizes

X for the raffle,
Y for everybody who succeeds

X = the grand prize (raffle)

  • Free membership for X months (my fave)
  • X # free months for fam & friends
  • Secret santa combo
  • Major swag
  • Cash

The emails on this page assume the grand prize is something like "6 months of free membership." Adjust the custom value if you go a different way.

Y = what everyone who succeeds gets

Perceived value roughly = or > the entry fee. High margin, high perceived value, low hard cost.

  • Gym hoodie or a "Project Zero finisher" shirt
  • Swag + supps bundle
  • A free month of an upgrade (nutrition, open gym, etc.)
  • For new folks: the challenge fee back over 12 months on a membership
Sizing it

Play the numbers: buy-in × projected participation should comfortably cover Y for everyone who succeeds (assume most of them will) plus the grand prize. If you're giving free membership as X, your hard cost is basically zero if you're a group training gym. Zero out of pocket cost + high perceived value = a win in our book. 

05 Pricing

Internal and external

Internal (members)

  • $50-100 [play the numbers based on the optimal intersection b/w prize, buy-in, and projected participation #'s]
  • Waived with referral
  • Optional upgrade for lower tiered members for a fee

External (new folks)

I like contrasting offers incentivising memberships but it's your call.

  • Find your weekly billing rate for your avg membership and multiply by 7-10x.
  • $69/wk membership → $499-699 challenge. $99/wk membership? $699-$999 challenge.
  • The lower you go on the challenge the less likely people are to sign up for the membership right away and vice versa.
How the contrast works at the close

The outsider sees three options on the pricing sheet: the intensive one-on-one (the pricing anchor big number you throw out), the challenge on its own, and the challenge included w/ a membership. The membership looks like the obvious move because you priced the challenge that way on purpose. Script in section 11.

06 The Guarantee

Project Zero Guarantee
outsiders only

Read it exactly like this at the close, then stop talking. Fill in the blanks before your staff meeting so everybody says it the same way.

Owner / closer, right before the options

"If you attend at least [ X # of sessions], submit your holiday habits weekly check-in every week, and do your one-on-ones with your coach on weeks [2, 4, and 6], and you're either not happy with your progress or you want to screw over [self-deprecating joke here] we'll give you 100% of your money back AND give you an extra $100 for your trouble. Best case you've made more progress on what your New Year's resolution was gonna be in the next 6 weeks than you did all year last year… worst case you leave with some new friends, some bad jokes, and an extra $100 in your pocket to start up with whatever else you'd do if this wasn't with us in January." [close]

What you're committing to

  • Attendance count: your booking software already has it. Don't build anything.
  • Weekly holiday habits check-in: use the Habit Tracker (Canva). Printed, they hand it to a coach each week. Or a one-question text reply. Pick one.
  • One-on-ones weeks 2, 4, 6 (2, 4, 6, 8 if you went eight weeks): 10-15 min w/ their coach. Book all three on day 1. Again, not necessary to include in your guarantee but can be a nice touch. 

Who it's for

  • Outsiders paying the external price. Members already have skin in the game w/ the buy-in.
  • It's a different bar than winning the challenge. Somebody can gain a pound, hit every box above, and still be covered.
  • For more info on this see the Ultimate Guarantee Masterclass in Circle.
07 Promotion

One email or FB post
does not = a "launch"

Multiple, multiple reminders. People love to forget.

Internal (your members)

  • Email/text member list (copy in section 08)
  • Sign-up sheet in gym (can be as simple as a big highlighted section of your whiteboard)
  • Announce at every session (script below)
  • Member's FB group / online community
  • Multiple, multiple reminders

Referrals

  • $50 off first month's membership or PZero pricing
  • Member's buy-in is waived when their friend signs up (challenge or membership) before you start
  • Make sure your team knows how to talk about the referral deal. Entry fee is waived IF that person refers someone who signs up for an approved program, so be clear on what counts (membership, challenge, or both) so you don't have to awkwardly walk anything back.

Past customers / VIP list

  • Personalized message/video similar to the member copy (potentially offer for free). Section 10.

Cold traffic

  • Organic posts announcing challenge on your various platforms [focus on the problem and the prizes!]
  • Ads [3WTW ads w/ Project Zero headlines are solid]

Announce at sessions · every session, starting 2 weeks out

Coach, before warm-up

"Quick one before we get started. We're running Project Zero in the gym for the holidays. The big idea - most people gain significant weight over the holidays and spend the New Year working off the weight they gained the last six weeks of the year. We want to make sure that doesn't happen this year.

So the challenge is super simple: don't gain weight between [START DATE] and the end of the year and you win. Weigh in on day one, weigh in on the last day, that's it. Everybody who doesn't gain gets [Y] and goes in the drawing for [GRAND PRIZE]. It's [BUY-IN] to sign up, or it's free if you bring in somebody who signs up before we start. Sign-up sheet's on the whiteboard, and if you've got any questions just grab me after!"

Individual sign-up convos

Ask if they have someone they're planning on bringing in to get the entry fee waived: "Perfect, have you got someone you're bringing in to get the buy-in waived, or do you just want us to charge the card on file?"

The desk line

Every member, every day until registration closes: "You in for Project Zero?" Assume they didn't read the email. Write the name on the sheet in front of them.

Get clear on the numbers

Every coach says [BUY-IN], [GRAND PRIZE], [Y] and the referral deal the exact same way. Two people quoting different numbers is how you lose a sale you already had. Put them all on the cheat sheet (section 14) and have the team read them out loud at the staff meeting.

11 The Close

The offer script

For outsiders (and past members) after the consult. Pull out the 3-option sheet that fits the client. Read the guarantee (section 06) right before this if you're running it.

  1. [pull out 3-option sheet that fits client] "Okay great so based on everything we've talked about today we've got a couple of options that I think could be a great fit for you:" [if standard, non mega-ticket offer point to big number on right] "so we can throw this one completely out the door, that's our intensive, one-on-one program and we just straight don't need that in your case."
  2. "Based on your situation we have either of these two options that would be the best fit for you: [OPTION 1 PROJECT ZERO PAID IN FULL] includes [XYZ] and the investment for that is [FULL PROJECT ZERO PRICE], and [OPTION 2 MEMBERSHIP] includes [ABC + XYZ] and the investment for that is just [MEMBERSHIP PRICE BILLED WEEKLY]and you get all the benefits of Project Zero for free."
  3. "Which of those options makes the most sense to you?" Then stop talking.
  4. If yes (to either A or B)→ CELEBRATE WITH THEM, take their card, book their first session as soon as possible, give them a parting gift as a bonus, and serve the heck out of them!
  5. If no → move to objections (IF I'M TELLING THE TRUTH SHOULD BE FIRST MOVE 99.999% OF THE TIME)

How the two options usually look

  • Option 1: Project Zero on its own at the external price.
  • Option 2: Project Zero included w/ a membership (weekly billing, challenge rolled in). Priced so this one is the no-brainer.

After the challenge

  • Anybody on Option 1 gets a conversation in the last week, before the weigh-out, with the "fee back over 12 months" credit on the table if you chose it.
  • Or set the challenge to auto-renew into weekly billing w/ a reminder, for the folks who don't sign up for a membership right away.
