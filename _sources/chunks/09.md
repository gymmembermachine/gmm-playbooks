## 🖤 **Black Friday Game Plan (Even If You Waited 'Til Now)**

We'll make this simple - if your churn's under 5% — skip the fancy funnels, crazy discounts, and "buy my Black Friday that doesn't work" goo-roo nonsense.

In gyms like ours, simple, curiosity-driven messaging tends to outperform everything else when it comes to Black Friday.

Your most powerful move here is typically going to be a reactivation (with "never been" and "used to be" clients). Details on that below, but first, let's talk about timelines & ads…

### RE: Timeline

Black Friday has shifted earlier and earlier in the month these days. If you need or want to do something earlier in the month it can absolutely be a viable strategy.

### RE: Ads

There are essentially 3 situations you'll probably find yourself in:

1. **Have an offer that's currently working for you and you don't really care to change things?** KEEP RUNNING IT! Maaaybe talk to your CS team in Slack and see if the squad thinks adding Black Friday "wrappers" might keep things rolling for you, but otherwise, if something's working, keep doing it!
2. **Have an offer that works but want to do something for Black Friday anyway?** Again, the most effective answer here *tends *to be adding Black Friday "wrappers" to whatever's currently working for you. Just let the team know in Slack and we'll get those up for you ASAP!
3. **Need something new, period?** We got you! Talk to the squad in Slack about best options for this time of year. The most effective answer *tends *to be a "curiosity" Black Friday offer (e.g. an ad without mentioning specific discounts or parameters) but other options *can *work depending on you, your gym, and your market. Hit us up in Slack and we'll get you up and running with the new thing long before the turkey coma hits.
