===== LESSON 2295923 | section 674164 (Tech Help [How to send your Plug & Play campaigns]) | Step 1: Setting up your list(s)
URL: https://gmm.circle.so/c/plug-play-marketing-calendar/sections/674164/lessons/2295923
FEATURED VIDEO: none

- Go to Contacts → More Filters to begin building your list
- Make sure to add this criteria for any reactivation you launch
- Phone is not empty
- Email is not empty
- DND is not enabled for SMS
- DND is not enabled for Email
- Add additional filters to exclude members
- i.e. Tag is NOT: member, active membership, etc.
- Once you add all necessary filters, click Save as smart list and title it so you know it's the one you want to use for the campaign you're looking to launch

===== LESSON 2295925 | section 674164 (Tech Help [How to send your Plug & Play campaigns]) | Step 2: Adjusting your workflows
URL: https://gmm.circle.so/c/plug-play-marketing-calendar/sections/674164/lessons/2295925
FEATURED VIDEO: none

- Go to Automation and find the Plug and Play Marketing Calendar folder
- Go to the subfolder of the month you're looking to launch in and click the reactivation workflow i.e. *Reactivation: Dads Train Free June *or *August Back to School Challenge: Members*
- Once you click the workflow, this will bring you to the workflow builder/editor
- If you do not want to make any edits to the copy - all you need to do is switch the workflow from draft to publish and click Save
- Once the workflow is published and saved, it is ready to be launched!
- If you do make edits, make sure to save each action you edit and save the entire workflow to keep any changes you make
- Once you are done making changes, switch the workflow from Draft to Publish
- Click Save and now your workflow is active and ready to be launched

===== LESSON 2295928 | section 674164 (Tech Help [How to send your Plug & Play campaigns]) | Step 3: Launching your campaigns
URL: https://gmm.circle.so/c/plug-play-marketing-calendar/sections/674164/lessons/2295928
FEATURED VIDEO: none

- Go to Contacts and click the Smartlist you created in step 1
- Select all contacts on the page and then check the Select All box to select the entire list
- Click the Add to Automation button ▶️
- Action Name: Add to reactivation
- Automation Type: Workflow
- Workflow: (select the reactivation campaign you want to launch i.e. *Reactivation: Dads Train Free June)*
- *Send in **drip mode**
- Select desired start date and time
- Batch Quantity: 2
- Repeat After: 1 Minutes
- Send on: Select desired days i.e. Monday-Friday
- Process from: select desired start and end times i.e. Start 9AM, End 6PM
