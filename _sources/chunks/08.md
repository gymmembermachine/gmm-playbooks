### 2. Back to School Challenge

**The audience**

✅ Never been clients

✅ Past clients

✅ Current clients

## **The Concept**

This is obviously a layer up in both complexity and yes, potential benefit... in short - rocking a challenge (I like six weeks personally, but you could go anywhere from 4-8 here) that promote to current, past, and never-been members.

My favorite "format" for this is the old "five ways to win" play that allows you collect new cash in 5 ways:

1. Current members ($50-$150 'buy in' for the challenge, waived if they refer someone)
2. Referrals
3. Past members
4. Reactivations of your unconverted clients
5. Cold traffic

**I unpack the full details of how a 5 ways to win play works ****[here](https://gmm.circle.so/c/project-zero/sections/668588/lessons/2535013)**, and you'll just apply that concept to a Back to School challenge video above.

The biggest downside to these types of programs is the added logistics of tracking, winning criteria, etc. Honestly, it's not always worth it, but if you're looking for something outside of the typical % bodyfat loss challenge I've added some fun ideas below.

**POTENTIAL WINNING CRITERIA**

Some of my favorite challenges were always the habit/input based challenges that felt more fair than a performance or body comp improvement driven challenge.

If you're looking for more thematic ideas, *I'd recommend picking 2-3 of these with a simple tracker sheet as a scoreboard (sample tracker to make it as simple as possible for you below).*

📍1. Perfect Attendance (Most Sessions Attended)

Goal: Track every visit during the challenge

⸻

📍2. Referral/Hall Pass Champ (Most Referrals)

Goal: Track who brings in the most new leads during the challenge

⸻

📍3. Homework Hero (Habit Tracker / Outside Tasks)

Goal: Award points for completing daily/weekly habits like hydration, food logging, walks

Bonus: Send weekly point summary or "report card" email

⸻

📍4. Best School Spirit (Social Sharing / Gym Swag)

Goal: Track who tags you on IG, posts photos, wears gear

⸻

📍5. Valedictorian (Overall "Report Card")

Goal: Track combined stats: attendance, referrals, habits, attitude

Bonus: Display leaderboard weekly via email or post in a Facebook Group

### The Deets

Step 1: Decide if you'd rather do this or the simple reactivation (don't take the decision lightly!)

Step 2: Decide on dates that make sense (I like 2 weeks of promotion starting between 1-3 weeks after school starts with the six week challenge rolling after that. For some of you this might be late August, others might push to mid-September.

Step 3: Decide on prize(s), winning criteria, and price (use video for reference on these items)

Step 4: Prep team for details (logistics, offer specifics, launch timing for follow-up, conversion mechanism off back end [I like a simple conversation here but you could also rock an autorenewal to weekly billing with a reminder for those that don't sign up for a membership right away)

Step 5: Promote

Step 6: Execute like hell!

### Promotion

*As always please adjust the copy to fit your voice, challenge variation, timing, etc. Note - we didn't add specific copy regarding challenge criteria because many of you will likely choose different criteria, but feel free to add notes about your winning criteria to the copy below before launching (particularly if it's an input driven challenge - I find that sounds more "doable" to the average person than a best transformation/weight loss/etc. type challenge). It also happens to be easier to add context and talk through winning criteria than to write novels in (already long) emails, so we went with the options below as the default.*

*We made the assumption that the most typical grand prize would be a free membership for six months (we've found it to be the sweet spot for high value "excitement" that brings some real juice to your promotion without giving away the farm unnecessarily with a bunch of cash or other hard cost prizes). As always, adjust for your situation as you see fit.*

### **CURRENT CLIENTS**

- Email/text member list
- Sign-up sheet in gym (can be as simple as a big highlighted section of your whiteboard)
- Announce at every session
- Member's FB group/online community
- Multiple, multiple reminders - people love to forget and one email or FB post does not = a "launch"
