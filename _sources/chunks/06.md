⛹️‍♂️ **Athletes/Youth/Sports Performance**

Summer is an amazing time to take advantage of kids being home (and parents desperately needing something for them to do).

If you *don't *already train athletes, then this is probably not worth exploring on such short notice. Training kids and giving them a great experience can be VERY different than training adults, and training younger kiddos is VERY different than training a driven high school athlete and vice versa.

In short, adding this population can do a lot to take your eye off the ball (if your main biz is gen pop fitness) and likely isn't worth the time spent staffing, structuring, and servicing the program if it's not something you plan to continue to do for the long haul.

That said, if you DO already train athletes/kids, then leverage the hell out of this time of year.

I'd hit the email list, promote internally to your members, and yes, if you've got capacity, run ads for your summer sports performance programs (we've got a number of offers and ad variations that have worked great for this population).

Before we finalize the schedule, I wanted to check in with you first:

**[line for parents of athletes currently training with you] **Would you be interested in adding more sessions for your athlete this summer?

===== LESSON 2395931 | section 632951 (July) | July Summary

July's an interesting time of the year here in the states...

You're in the last 1-2 months before school starts back up, travel is heavy on the calendar for a lot of folks, and you're sort of in between the more major notches on the fitness calendar (New Year, Back to School, Pre-summer transformations, etc.).

We always did two "major" things for July:

1. We kept running our normal evergreen plays (for us this was typically a 30-day intro program) and crushed with it.
2. We had some fun on 4th of July with themed workouts and a gym hang people could bring their friends and family to.

In your case, you're (hopefully) working on converting a bunch of guys from the Dads Train Free promo last month and making hay on that (again, offense is the best defense) and your members are doing their thing, referring members, and sticking around with the Summer Bingo Play.

If you didn't start the Summer Bingo last month, you can absolutely still refer to last month's edition and roll that out now (seriously, do it!).

The other big notch on the timeline I'd recommend you think about is 4th of July - it's a great excuse to have a gym hang/BBQ and what we've found over the years is that it's often easier to get clients to bring a referral to a "hang" than it is to bring that referral to a "session" as an introduction to you and your gym community.

Showing up to a hangout gives prospects a chance to get to know you and your gym community, with super low stakes, no pressure of a sale, no anxiety around looking like an idiot in front of experienced gym goers, etc. etc.

My "break in case of emergency" thought for July (e.g. your evergreen plays aren't cutting it and your event didn't pull the way you wanted it to) is to go back to the well on a super low barrier offer. Bonus points if you combine it with some local networking to connect with other local businesses that are dealing with some of the same summer issues you are (more context unpacked in the video.
