We don't believe in slow months and pity parties.

Yes, some of your population may be going on vacation.

Yes, some of your clients may use this as a convenient excuse to cancel.

That said, there's a reason our best gyms thrive and, at times, even make the summer the best part of their year (and I'm not just talking about the obvious summer jumps for sports performance gyms).

That reason?

Offense!

Offense is the best defense, and the best offense in this case is to get *aggressive *with offers (read: giving folks a reason to get to the gym) and then creating compelling reasons to drive engagement inside the gym.

This month we've got two primary things that I think fit for most gyms (outside of your ad plays) plus some additional considerations you may want to kick around to take your summer "slowdown" and flip it on its ugly, acne-ridden head.

## OTHER Considerations RE: Dads Train Free

**The Sniper option: **I looooove super high touch "sniper" reactivations where you send a personalized message to specific former clients who were a great fit for your gym. The best practice here is to send a personalized video message from you or the trainer they worked most closely with inviting them back for the offer (in this case, dads train free).

**Getting some cash up front: **While I don't recommend pulling punches on this offer (e.g. "you can train once a week for free and if you want more you have to pay ← DON'T DO THIS!) there's a world where if you have multiple tiers of services at your gym (say small group AND 1-1 or a primary training-only offer but you *also *sell nutrition coaching or supplements) you could conceivably offer a 3 times per week membership for free but offer situational upgrades during your consult with them to collect some cash up front.

*Don't* turn this into a pitchfest or forget to keep the main thing (converting a high percentage of these guys into long term members) the main thing, but if you've got some great no-pressure closers that can read the room, talk through goals, and *only *pitch the upgrades when it makes perfect sense for the prospect, you could turn this otherwise delayed revenue play into something that makes some cash from the get-go.
