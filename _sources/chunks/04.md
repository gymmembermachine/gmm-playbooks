## The Concept

Run a simple event for your gym that:

- Doesn't require a crazy budget
- Gives your members a fun thing to do (and makes them feel like you care about more than just their workouts)
- Gives your members a chance to connect with folks they don't train with
- Gives your members an easy way to introduce friends to you and your gym community
- Gives past members an easy way to slide back into the community
- Gives you a great excuse to connect with other local business owners and their employees

## The Deets

These will vary depending on what you want to do and how big you want to go - could be as simple as a gym BBQ or potluck, or as "advanced" as Mark's PSTS picnic where he goes all out every summer with food trucks, bounce houses, etc.

If I'm slapping something together relatively quickly I'm probably finding someone who can smoke some delicious meats (yes... I just wanted to write delicious meats...), grabbing some sides, snacks, and beverages at the store, and just having a nice free hang with members.

*Asking members if they know anyone with a food truck or brewery connection is a great way to get some help/get an in that you wouldn't have had otherwise** **

RE Dates - you could always do it either the morning of or the Saturday of the 4th here in the states if you move on it *now*, but any weekend of the month will do.

Note: you can absolutely make this a group workout + hang if your model sets up nicely for it, but you'll probably actually get more outside attendance if it's just a nice hang.

## To Offer or Not to Offer?

If it's me I'm making a nice short speech thanking everyone for hanging out and letting any guests know that they can get a free (X) pass either via QR code or by texting the gym number.

1. Thank members
2. Shout out your team and force a round of applause
3. Thank guests
4. No pressure offer with simple CTA
5. Enjoy the party

The end goal here is to make guests feel like you guys are a great gym community, not to make them feel like they just fed shitty brisket in exchange for a time share presentation.

## **MEMORIAL DAY MELTDOWN (Flash Class)**

### **The Play**

One special 60–75 min class, paid add-on, patriotic hero-WOD theme. Built to feel bigger than a normal class. Should have decorations, playlist, photo ops, optional cookout/joint ventures tabling after.

### **Pricing Model**

**Members: **$25–35

Feels like a "perk unlock" with discount in comparison to non-members

**Non-members: **$45–55

Use as soft sell for membership

**With T-shirt bundle: **+$15

**Bring-a-friend bundle: **2 for $45

### **Class Format Options (Pick One)**

1. **The Murph** — classic hero WOD (1 mile run, 100 pullups, 200 pushups, 200 squats, 1 mile run — scalable). Highest emotional resonance and ***not great for our demo***.
2. **22 in 22** — 22 stations, 22 reps each, honoring the "22 veterans a day" statistic ***[This is my personal favorite]***
3. **Red, White & Blue Team WOD** — break the gym into 3 teams, team scoring, fun competition.

### **Cap & Scarcity**

- **Cap at 30–40 spots.** Post the counter publicly ("12 spots left").
- Open registration 3 weeks out. Most sell-outs happen week-of.

### **Day-Of Experience Checklist**

- American flag decor, red/white/blue chalk on whiteboards
- Country/rock playlist (ask members to contribute songs in advance, engagement)
- Moment of silence or brief story about the workout's meaning
- Photographer or staff member on video duty
- Post-workout: protein shake station, watermelon, or partnership with local BBQ spot, any kind of Joint Venture play
- Group photo (THE shot for social & next year's promo)
