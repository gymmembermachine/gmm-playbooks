Ahhhh, August...

I used to be a part of a coaching group that called August the "death month"... probably mostly because of the high skew of athlete businesses in the group and their inevitable drop with the kiddos when they head back to school, but there were obviously still the additonal challenges of "last chance" vacations before school starts up again on the adult side as well...

Either way, by now you know our stance on bad months and pity parties

There's always a percentage of your market that has (or will allow) fitness to climb to the top of their to-do list for any number of reasons.

One of my favorite stories that backs this up is one of my OG clients - Ryan Wade in Pittsburgh, that shared the screenshot below waaaay back when we were just getting started:

![]()

It wasn't a fancy August offer.

It wasn't a drastic change in how he was doing things.

There wasn't any bait and switch BS.

It was a gym owner executing on the basics and *marketing aggressively even though all the gym owners he had been around talked about August like it was the stinkin' plague.*

Further evidence? Prior to June's release we talked about how everyone wants to say "summer slowdown", "summer's an awful time of year," etc.

Welp, as of the time of writing this (early July), we had:

- Mike hit a record of $91.9k monthly revenue in June (that's in ONE small group training location)
- Harrison hit a record of 32 signups in June
- The awesome folks at Alloy Fort Collins won the award for top club and signed up 28 new members last month
- Chuck had a record of 16 signups (most of those being higher ticket 1-1 clients

The point?

*The basics are the basics because they work.*

August is really no different - frankly I think the top thing you can do this time of year is put the blinders on, market what's working for you and your studio right now (our squad will already be adjusting for seasonal variations of your current offers), and run the hell out the play you've called.

But in case you're looking for some extra juice:

- Back to school (BTS) is on the way for many of you - this can be a great time for a simple reactivation of cancelled members or a more involved full-on "challenge." We'll unpack both options below.
- The "fanciest" thing you could potentially throw in at this time of year is a viral giveaway that leads into your BTS program (particularly powerful for those of you in markets that start up school a little later in the month, for those of you in Texas, for example, you'll be BAU by the time the end of the month hits)
- Seasons are in swing for the athlete businesses - so both an inseason offering for your fall sport kiddos and a preseason offering for your winter sport kids can do well here.
- If you didn't run a member appreciation event in July, be sure to check that video/play out from last month - there's still time!

## 1. Back to School Simple Reactivation

## **The audience**

✅ Never been clients

✅ Past clients

### The Concept

A simple excuse to get people in to see what you're all about - I like going super low barrier for uncoverted leads (particularly if we're not doing a "challenge"-esque promotion, so a nice 7-21 days free or a proven LBO (e.g. 14 or 21 days for $99) can be nice here.

Do this if you're not planning a big back-to-school challenge (either you're hyper anti-"challenge" OR you like the timing of a "Fall Back Into Fitness" in mid-September or October better than running something bigger now). We've got a bit of a nifty way to make your BTS challenge a little simpler in the next video, so feel free to check that out before you make the call on which way you want to go.

### The Deets

Step 1: Choose your offer. Martie will show you how to adjust this on the tech side but I like defaulting to 14 days free here. It's compelling, clear, and gives people an excuse to take action when it's way easier to nurse a glass of wine because they finally got the kids out of their hair (and they forgot how annoying pick-up and drop-off was).

Step 2: Build/clean up your lists. I like a never-been and a past client list here but you may want to pull some folks off your bulk past client list for some targeted video reactivation shenanigans for your VIPs.

Step 3: Review and adjust the copy below (already in your GMM/Ops automations if you're with us for your CRM ❤️

Step 4: Send it!

Step 5: Convert off the back end!
