## THE RUNDOWN
✅ Summer doesn't have to mean a slowdown… Normal = losing some members. Not normal = mass exodus. *Don't let convenient excuses allow us to feel good when our focus should be on "being good" (feeling good doesn't make the situation any better)...*

✅ Reward the behavior you want to see to incentivise engagement
- Referrals
- Reviews
- Attendance
- Attendance at a gym event
- Testimonials

✅ Prizes can be simple, could do drawing or everyone who completes wins

✅ Don't run it internally around an another internal challenge
