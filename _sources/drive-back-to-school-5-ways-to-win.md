## Winning Criteria
**STANDARD "SETTINGS"**
Outcome-driven traditional challenges e.g. Percent bodyfat or weight loss. Can be paired with or replaced with habit-driven challenges (e.g. attendance, check-in, or other input-driven challenges).

**FUN THEMATIC IDEAS** *I'd recommend picking 2-3 of these with a simple tracker sheet*
1. Perfect Attendance (Most Sessions Attended) - Goal: Track every visit during the challenge
2. Referral/Hall Pass Champ (Most Referrals) - Goal: Track who brings in the most new leads during the challenge
3. Homework Hero (Habit Tracker / Outside Tasks) - Goal: Award points for completing daily/weekly habits like hydration, food logging, walks. Bonus: Send weekly point summary or "report card" email
4. Best School Spirit (Social Sharing / Gym Swag) - Goal: Track who tags you on IG, posts photos, wears gear
5. Valedictorian (Overall "Report Card") - Goal: Track combined stats: attendance, referrals, habits, attitude. Bonus: Display leaderboard weekly via email or post in a Facebook Group

## Promotion
Internal
- Email/text member list
- Sign-up sheet in gym
- Announce at every session
- Member's FB group
- Multiple, multiple reminders - people love to forget and one email or FB post does not = a "launch"

Referrals
- $X off first month's membership or challenge pricing (typically $50 off if avg. membership <$250/m, $100 if above $250/m)

Past customers/VIP list
- Personalized message/video inviting them back at a discounted rate (or for free if you want to be hyper aggressive)

Your list
- Bulk reactivation and full launch w/ text & email campaigns
- Social media posts + stories promoting the challenge

Cold traffic
- Organic posts announcing challenge on your various platforms [focus on the problem and the prizes!]
- Ads [3WTW ads w/ Project Zero headlines are solid]

## Pricing
Internal
- $50-100 [play the numbers based on the optimal intersection b/w prize, buy-in, and projected participation #'s]
- Waived with referral
- Optional upgrade for lower tiered members for a fee

External
I like contrasting offers that incentivise memberships right away but it's your call.
To do this - find your weekly billing rate for your avg membership and multiply that weekly price by 7-10x to price your challenge at a rate that makes the membership a no-brainer e.g. if membership is $69/wk, then $599 nicely incentivises the membership option. $99/wk membership? $799 - $999 challenge. The lower you go on the challenge the less likely people are to sign up for the membership right away and vice versa.
