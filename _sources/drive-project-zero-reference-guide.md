**The Problem**
Holiday sales blues (need something to make money that's not JUST a nice PIFFY BF play)
External - harder to overcome objections of holiday schedules, holiday mindset, etc.
Internal - lower engagement, more freeze requests, cancellations (start back up in January), etc.

**The Solution**
An internal/external play to get your current members fired up, grab you an excuse to get some referrals, and a built-in overcome/holiday specific external promo to get new folks in the door.

**Summary - See course**

**Prize Recs**
HOLIDAY OFFER - Project Zero!
* internal only - engagement play $50 - all in a pot, winners split the pot (no profit to gym - booo)
* internal / external - [competitive/pot] Succeed? Name gets put in a raffle to win X [grand prize = secret santa combo, major swag, cash, free membership for x time, x # free months for fam & friends], and EVERYONE who succeeds will receive Y (where perceived value of Y is roughly = or > entry fee)
* alternative for new folks who succeed - challenge fee back, give it back over 12 months

**Winning Criteria**
Don't gain weight - you win/get put in the raffle*
*differs from guarantee criteria (outsiders-only)

**Promotion**
Internal
- Email/text member list
- Sign-up sheet in gym
- Announce at every session
- Member's FB group
- Multiple, multiple reminders - people love to forget and one email or FB post does not = a "launch"

Referrals
- $50 off first month's membership or PZero pricing

Your list
- Hey {firstname}, we're launching a [six/eight] week challenge in the gym for the holidays with some pretty incredible prizes - we call it "Project Zero!" Best part? Everyone can win! (if 100 people enter there can be 100 winners!) Interested? Let me know!

Past customers/VIP list
- Personalized message/video similar to above (potentially offer for free)

Cold traffic
- Organic posts announcing challenge on your various platforms [focus on the problem and the prizes!]
- Ads [3WTW ads w/ Project Zero headlines are solid]

**Pricing [+ deciding on the size of your prize]**
Internal
- $50-100 [play the numbers based on the optimal intersection b/w prize, buy-in, and projected participation #'s]
- Waived with referral
- Optional upgrade for lower tiered members for a fee

External - I like contrasting offers incentivising memberships but it's your call
- What's your weekly billing rate for your avg membership? Price your challenge at a rate that makes the weekly a no-brainer e.g. if membership is $69/wk, then $599 nicely incentivises the membership option. $99/wk membership? $999 challenge.

**Guarantee Recommendation**
Project Zero Guarantee: if you attend at least [2.5x/wk - 15 for 6 weeks, 20 for 8], submit your holiday habits weekly check-in every week, and do your one-on-ones with your coach on weeks 2, 4, and 6, and you're either not happy with your progress or you want to screw over [self-deprecating joke here] we'll give you 100% of your money back AND give you an extra $100 for your trouble. Best case you've made more progress on what your New Year's resolution was gonna be in the next 6 weeks than you did all year last year… worst case you leave with some new friends, some bad jokes, and an extra $100 in your pocket to start up with whatever else you'd do if this wasn't with us in January. [close]

**Offer Script**
[pull out 3-option sheet that fits client] Okay great so based on everything we've talked about here are the options that I think'll be the best fit for you: [if standard, non mega-ticket offer point to big number on right] so we can throw this one completely out the door, that's our intensive, one-on-one program and we just straight don't need that in your case.

Based on your situation we have either of these two options that would be the best fit for you - OPTION1PROGRAM NAME includes XYZ and the investment for that is ____, and OPTION2PROGRAM NAME includes ABC + XYZ and the investment for that is just ______.

Which of those options makes the most sense to you?

If yes → CELEBRATE WITH THEM, take their card, book their first session as soon as possible, give them a parting gift as a bonus, and serve the heck out of them!

If no → move to objections (IF I'M TELLING THE TRUTH SHOULD BE FIRST MOVE 99.999% OF THE TIME)
