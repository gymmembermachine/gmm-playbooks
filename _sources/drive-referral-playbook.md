### Win/Win/Win
The simplest "powerful" move in the referral game in my mind is creating win/win/win deals for referrer & referee. E.g. Give $X/Get $X
- Removes skeezy MLM feel (I'm only referring you b/c I get shit)
- Makes it valuable for referrer
- Makes it easy for referrer to talk about
- Gives referee an excuse to take action

I typically recommend Give 50/Get 50 if you're priced below $200, Give $100/Get $100 if you're priced above it, but you can play with what you like.

### NO WASTED Real Estate
Have your referral program EVERYWHERE: flyers in the gym near busy areas (Sample Flyer: https://www.canva.com/design/DAF7lP1QiIc/Cw1mn5Hy_SDM_ex0NEHxiQ/view), pinned posts in member FB groups, cover photo of member communities, on the waiver/agreement, email signature, "on the damn urinal."
BONUS: If you're doing this for the first time, make it a "thing" and position it as a temporary/test run deal (mmmm…. urgency). Announce it at sessions, post the flyers everywhere, make it a leaderboard for the month w/ a drawing for a prize, etc.

### Activation Points

### The Advice Script
"Could I get your advice on something?" [wait for response]
"I'm really trying to [GOAL e.g. fill up these sessions/grow this thing/etc.] and am wondering where the hell I can find more awesome clients/people like you!?" [wait for response]
INDIVIDUAL: "Oh sweet, I'd love to set up an intro if you're cool with it - NEXT ACTION" [Bonus points for a group selfie + 3-way text w/ you, client, and referee "Hey Sally this is my trainer I've been meaning to tell ya about - he said he's giving $100 off to friends of select clients and I thought of you! I'll let you guys take it from here :)"]
NOTE: Please understand that not everyone is going to be ready to refer, and if you're a giant jerk-off when they don't have anyone today you'll likely turn them off on referring forever. Don't be a jerkoff.

### Monthly/time limited referral contests/drawings
Simplest: Every referral = 1 entry, drawing at the end of the month for X prize
More complex: Every referral LEAD = 1 entry, every referral CLOSED = 5 entries, Frequent Sweaters = 1 entry, video testimonial = 1 entry, etc.

### Event-based asks
Your classic "Bring-A-Friend Day/Week/etc." My primary recommendation here is to make this a *big f***ing deal…* "Free class every Saturday" does NOT = a big deal… It's this quarter's "Bring a friend free week" and we only have two spots per session AND we're doubling your referral gifts for folks who come in that week!!! ← Promoted before every session, in your internal groups, your email list, etc. = a big f***ing deal

### Clear is kind: The Generic Toned-Down Gym Sample
Hey FIRSTNAME, thanks so much for thinking of us! First off, we're absolutely HONORED - we know it takes a ton of trust to be comfortable sharing a squad like us with your friends & family and it's a responsibility we don't take lightly.
There's two ways to introduce them to our universe:
OPTION 1: Just plug their info into YOURREFERRALURL and tell them to expect us to reach out so we can hook them up w/ their $100 intro discount.
OPTION 2: The simplest way to connect your friend with us is to click this link and start a three-way chat with OWNER, you, and your friend/family member/person you met on the street :)
Example Message: Hey <Referral Name>, I wanted to connect you with OWNER from GYMNAME! They're the guys that have been helping me get back in the gym (and actually love it, lol). Since I've joined GYMNAME I've <insert the benefits>. I wanted to introduce the two of you, so you can talk with OWNER directly if you have any questions about how the program works and how they might possibly help you as well. <Sign off>
COMMISSION: We know you're not doing this for the money, but we will comp you with $100 off your next month (and give your friend $100 off their first month with us) if they end up joining the program. You get paid, your friend gets a discount, and we don't have to pay Zuck the Suck - win, win, win!
