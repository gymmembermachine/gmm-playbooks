## THE PREP
- Set a date and time
- Decide format: workout or no workout
- Confirm location (gym lot, park, parking access, weather plan if outdoors)
- Food plan: BBQ, catered, potluck, food truck, etc.
- Drink plan: Water/non-alcoholic options; Alcohol (be smart here particularly if kids are invited); Kid drinks (juice, water bottles)
- Music plan: playlist, DJ, speakers
- Seating plan: tables, chairs, tents/shade if in gym lot and in TEXAS, BYOChair is great - just remember to note in invite
- Games/activities: yard games, etc.
- Waivers (if there's a workout or activities for guests)
- Staff & volunteer assignments: who's greeting, grilling, talking to guests, etc.

## THE PROMO
- CURRENT MEMBERS ****remember to allow them to invite friends and family****
  - Email (multiple invites + reminders day before and day of)
  - Text
  - Member communities (e.g. Facebook group)
  - Pre and post-session reminders (group)
  - Individual conversations
  - OPTIONAL: bring the most guests = win something fun (or drawing)
- PAST MEMBERS ****We miss you - come hang!****
  - Email; Text; Call/voicemail (optional)
- Local businesses (owner/manager + employees)
  - Neighbors; Potential future JV partners; Referral/Chamber of Commerce group members
  - Potential vendors: Food trucks, DJ, Breweries, Etc.

## THE EXECUTION
- If planning a workout - assign coaches & prep for big numbers. *Program for scalability, safety, simplicity, and fun*
- Set up tables, signage, check-in/RSVP/welcome table
- Staff or coach assigned to welcome new faces (non-members)
- Clearly mark where to get food/drinks (or tell staff to direct folks)
- Run a short welcome speech (thank you, shoutouts, offer e.g. 2 weeks free)
- Take lots of photos & videos (assign someone or hire)
- Feature members, guests, and good vibes on social
- Create a "Guest Interest Sheet" or QR code to claim free pass
- Give out branded swag or fun giveaways
- Clean-up plan: staff roles + post-event trash/recycling

## THE FOLLOW UP
- Send thank-you text or email to everyone who attended
- Post recap photos/video on social
- Email/text non-members who attended → "Thanks for coming! Want to claim that free 2-week pass?"
- Ask members to tag their friends who came in photos
- Schedule consults for interested guests
- Run follow-up promo: "July Jumpstart – for friends who came to the BBQ"
