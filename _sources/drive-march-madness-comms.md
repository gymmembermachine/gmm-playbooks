# PRELAUNCH

### TALKING POINTS

**Announce at sessions:** This will vary based on your prize selection and overall structure (paid vs. free, etc.) but make sure you're having trainers announce this at every session, have a public sign-up list on the whiteboard, etc.

The more fun, exciting, and enthusiastic your coaching team is about this the more members you're gonna get signed up and the more potential referrals you'll get by the end of the program.

"Quick announcement before we get started - we're bringing March "Member" Madness to the gym for you guys - it's a super fun team-based challenge where you'll get points for showing up, dropping a review for the gym, etc. and compete against other teams to win some pretty awesome prizes - it's just $__ to sign up or it's free if you bring someone in who signs up before the challenge starts, if you're interested you can plug your name on the sign up sheet here and if you have any questions just let me know!

**Individual sign-up convos:** Ask if they have someone they're planning on bringing in to get the entry fee waived. E.g. "Perfect, have you brought someone else in to sign up and join your squad or do you just want us to charge the card on file?"

**Getting clear on referral comms:** Make sure your team knows how to talk about the referral deal if they're going to be having these convos. Entry fee for current member is waived IF that person refers someone who *signs up* for an approved "program." For most of you that might be your membership, others might have a dedicated front-end offer for referred prospects, just make sure it's clear to your team (and your members) so you don't have to awkwardly walk anything back.

**Make brackets fun & public:** Announce & congratulate winning teams, consider micro-prizes for every week or shoutouts in member digital communities or via email, the more you incentivise and reward "wins" the more effort you'll get toward winning (and in this case winning = positive behaviors that impact you & and your gym)
