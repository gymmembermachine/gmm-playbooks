## PLUG THE LEAKY BUCKET: RETENTION = REVENUE
If you know a storm is coming it's a hell of a lot easier to prepare for it. So if we know the holidays are tougher for churn, *what are we going to do?*
- Project Zero (or other similar plays to incentivize engagement)
- Education plus…
- Coaching conversations (think proactive goal reset convos *now* so they're not desperation conversations later)

## REACTIVATIONS
Concept 1: There are diamonds in our own backyards… *they just need an excuse that's easy to say yes to when fitness jumps back up to the top of their to-do list.*
Concept 2: make it as easy as possible to say yes to while still being responsible for the business
- Free Trial: Standard; Rest of month free (find an excuse) - nice in December for Jan signups, nice for Mother/Father's Day. *Find an excuse or create one*
- Transparency Reactivation: https://gmm.circle.so/c/project-zero/sections/364606/lessons/1355731
- 5W2W: Project zero works nicely here (Internal buy in waived if member refers / Referrals / Sniper list (past members) / Database RA / Cold/new traffic)
- Members/Past Members: Birthday offer (free month for past member, free upgrade for current member; SMS/Email/Direct mail)

## New & Current Clients
Buying = opportunities. Highest emotional state = when they buy. Make it a conversation (e.g. "most folks like to bring a friend to train w/ them - each new member (that's you!) gets two free 7-day passes for friends & family - who'd you like to invite?") *don't get lazy and just make it a line on your agreement.* If you do nothing else on this massive sheet - DO THIS!

THE HOLIDAY LETTER + GIFT CARD/GIFT OF FITNESS
1. Letter from you to your members (hand sign each one, if you've got a big team let them sign them)
2. Attach a nice gift card w/ dollar value & expiration date clearly stated for them to give as gifts. Ideally send w/ a nice gift card envelope that's easy to give to a friend or a family member. Include the policy (new clients only) nicely in the letter.
3. Send. Ideally around Thanksgiving - 1st week of December.
4. Follow the F up - remind folks of the deadline for their friends and family to use their gift cards

BRING A FRIEND DAY/WEEK: Make it an event w/ a signup list or clear procedure; promote EVERYWHERE internally; train your team (confirmation, welcome, pre/post workout convos & offers, onboarding). I like it as a special event (a couple times a quarter at most) e.g. "Bring Your Boo" for October/Halloween cc: Connor Flahive

## Internal Challenges/Monthly Themes & Contests
You can go as "simple" as using Project Zero in Nov/Dec. Beware: complexity squeeze that doesn't match the output "juice"; confusing your members; doing the same shit all the time.

## Connecting with connectors
Free Month for the Owner ("Local outreach program"); Gift Cards for Clients of the business you're connecting with (nice cards, start w/ ~10, play to the ego, follow up); Viral Giveaway/Cross Promotion (see VG Play in Circle); Local Podcast; NEXTDOOR (super quick, easy play to run 2-4 times a year).
